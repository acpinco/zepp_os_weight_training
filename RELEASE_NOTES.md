# Release notes

What's new in Weight Lifting, newest first. You can see which version you have in the Zepp phone app's list of apps.

## 0.4.92

- Export and import on the phone (Zepp app, Weight Lifting, Export and import). Your workout history and workout plans are saved together in one backup file. Tap Show as text to copy it or email it to yourself; where your phone allows it, Share and Copy buttons appear too.
- To import, tap Import a backup, paste the backup into the large box, then tap Import. You can also give the web address of a backup instead. Workouts and plans you already have are kept as they are, so importing the same file twice adds nothing, and Undo the last import takes it all away again.

## 0.4.91

- Each workout plan on the phone's Workout Plans page now has Delete beside it, just like Workout History: tap Delete, then Sure? to remove the plan. You can still delete a plan from inside it too.
- The Workout Plans page on the phone has larger text. Making a new plan now starts with an Enter plan name button. Type the name, tap OK, and it shows with Next under it to go on to adding exercises. A plan's own page has Change plan name.
- The exercise list on the phone (when adding to a plan, or changing a set's exercise) has larger text and colour: exercises are green buttons, muscle groups pale green.
- Buttons on the phone that open something (Workout Plans, Workout History, each workout, each plan and each muscle group) have a large › on the right, and Workout Plans and Workout History stand out with larger names.
- A plan's page is tidier: each exercise has its name, then one row with its sets (− and +), ↑ and ↓ to move it, and ✕ to remove it.

## 0.4.90

- Home now shows how many free workouts you have left ("12 free workouts left"), so you always know before the app asks to be unlocked. Once it is unlocked, the line goes away.
- History shows 20 workouts at a time, on the watch and on the phone, with Next page and Previous page buttons to see the rest, so it opens quickly however many workouts you have.
- If you leave the app in the middle of a workout and do not come back to it for 30 minutes, the watch stops measuring your heart rate in the background, to save the battery. Opening the app again picks the workout up where you left it, and heart rate starts again.
- Fixed: tapping Disconnect on the phone's Strava box while a workout was uploading could leave Strava connected, or say it needed reconnecting.

## 0.4.89

- Workout plans. Make a plan on your phone (Zepp app, Weight Lifting, Workout Plans): tap New plan, give it a name, then choose the exercises you want to do and how many sets of each. On the watch, GO now asks whether to Quick start (as before) or Choose plan.
- With a plan, the exercise list starts with Remaining Exercises: each exercise with how many sets are left. Once all its sets are done it moves to the bottom of that list and is crossed out, and you can still pick it for another set.
- After the last planned set of an exercise, the rest screen's Start goes straight to the next exercise of the plan, in the order you set on the phone. You can change the order there with Move up and Move down.
- A tidier phone page: the disclaimer comes first and folds away once you accept it (tap it to read it again), Strava takes one line once connected, and your workout plans and workout history each have a page of their own.
- On the phone, the Back and Cancel buttons at the top of a screen now stay in place while you scroll, so they are always easy to find.
- Fixed: connecting Strava on a fresh install could show an error under "Connected to Strava" until the first workout uploaded.
- When you finish the last set of a plan, the watch says "Plan complete" with the plan's name. Choose Continue to keep lifting without the plan, or End to finish the workout.
- The workout summary (on the watch, the phone and Strava) names the plan, says where you did fewer sets than planned, and lists the exercises you skipped. You can finish early at any time, as before.

## 0.4.88

- You now get 14 free workouts before the app asks to be unlocked, up from 7. If you had already reached 7, you can start workouts again until you reach 14.

## 0.4.87

- The rest screen now shows how many sets you have done in the workout ("Sets 3"), and the green button says which set of that exercise is next, for example "Start 2nd Set of Dumbbell curl".
- The set number on the set screen and the Weight & reps screen now counts sets of that exercise, so your first squats after three sets of curls are Set 1, not Set 4.

## 0.4.86

- New Settings page, opened from Home, with the Isometric (Holds) Timer sound and a new Screen on setting. It opens once the first time you start the app after installing or updating, so you know they are there.
- The Isometric (Holds) Timer now vibrates by default. If you had picked Voice, Beeps or Silent, your choice is kept.
- Screen on: between sets the screen now stays on for as long as set on the watch (Display > Screen-on Duration > General), or you can choose 30 seconds to 3 minutes.
- During a set the screen always stays on, whatever this setting. The watch only sends motion readings while the screen is on, so reps would stop being counted, and it closes the app soon after the screen goes off, which would stop the 30-second cues during a hold.
- Fixed: on the workout summary, pressing the top right button once now presses Done whenever Done is on screen.

## 0.4.85

- New exercises: Machine shoulder press (Shoulders) and Machine seated row (Back).

## 0.4.84

- Heart rate now keeps tracking when the screen turns off, so it shows right away when you look again.

## 0.4.83

- Now works on square watches, such as the Amazfit Active 2 (Square).
