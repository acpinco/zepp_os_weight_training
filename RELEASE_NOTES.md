# Release notes

What's new in Weight Lifting, newest first. Your app version is shown in the Zepp phone app's list of apps.

## 0.4.83

- **Square watches, such as the Amazfit Active 2 (Square).** The app now runs on square screens as well as round ones. Every screen is scaled to fit the watch and centred, so it looks the same as on a round watch, just sized for yours. The watch's own status bar is hidden while the app is open to leave room for it.
- Not yet checked: moving around with the physical buttons on watches that have only two of them, such as the Active 2 (Square). Touch works everywhere. If the buttons don't do what you expect, please [report it](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
