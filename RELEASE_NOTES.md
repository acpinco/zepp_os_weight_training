# Release notes

What's new in Weight Lifting, newest first. You can see which version you have in the Zepp phone app's list of apps.

## 0.4.114

- Cancelling a subscription is easier. Support on the watch now has a second code: scan it to sign up, or, once subscribed, to manage or cancel your subscription in a tap, with nothing to sign in to. The link in your receipt email still works too.
- Prices now include tax, so you pay the price you see.
- If you bought the app, your watch now checks your purchase with your phone each time it syncs, rather than about once a month.

## 0.4.113

- During a set there is now a Back button under End set, for when you started the wrong exercise. Tap it twice to drop the set and go back to the rest screen, where you can change the exercise. The watch's back button still does the same. Finish is no longer on this screen; finish from the rest screen.
- The rest between sets now buzzes every 30 seconds (once at 30 seconds, twice at a minute), so you can time your rest without looking, even with the screen off (when Heart rate with screen off is on in Settings). It has its own setting, Rest Timer, in Settings: Vibrate or Off.
- The Personal best screen now stays up for 10 seconds, so there is time to read it, and its title and bars no longer run into the edge of the screen or the Next button.

## 0.4.112

- Behind-the-scenes tidying. The workout screens now redraw their numbers only when they change, and the Train safely and Heart rate screens start their text at the top.

## 0.4.111

- If you bought the app, your watch now checks your purchase with your phone about once a month, when it syncs. Nothing changes while it is paid for; a purchase moved to another phone stops working on the old one.
- Restoring a purchase on a new phone now emails you a 6-digit code to enter, so only the person who paid can move it.
- If you paid on the payment page without scanning the code on your watch first (from a shared or saved link), Restore on your watch now finds your purchase.
- On the phone, the Strava box now has Strava's own orange Connect with Strava button and the Compatible with Strava mark.
- When the Unlock screen cannot check for your payment, it now says why in plain words, such as your phone not being online.
- The Unlock screen and the payment pages now say where to email if something is not working: support@avourlabs.com.
- The app's version is now shown at the top of Settings and on the Unlock screen on the watch, and at the top of the phone page, so you can give it when asking for help.
- A new Support button on the watch's Home screen, and the Unlock screen, show a code to scan for the support page, with the app and its version already filled in.

## 0.4.110

- Workouts sent to Strava now say which watch they were done on, such as "Logged on Amazfit Active 3 Premium".
- On the phone, choosing an exercise has a Find by first letter list at the top. Pick a letter to see every exercise that starts with it, with no typing.
- In a workout plan on the phone, tapping an exercise's number now opens a list of the places it can move to, instead of the keyboard.
- In Workout History on the phone, each set of a workout has a Delete set button under it, for a set logged by mistake. Tap it, then tap Sure? to delete. The change goes to the watch at its next sync.

## 0.4.109

- The watch celebrates your personal bests. When a set is the heaviest you've ever lifted on that exercise, or the most reps you've done at your heaviest weight, a Personal best screen shows what you beat before the rest screen. It also counts the most reps for exercises without weight and your longest hold. The watch buzzes twice, and the screen goes on by itself after 3 seconds, or press the top right button to go on right away. Under the numbers, small bars show how that exercise went in your last few workouts, with today's in yellow on the right.

## 0.4.108

- New on the phone page: Exercise Progress. Pick an exercise to see a line graph of how it has gone, workout by workout. Switch between your heaviest set and the total weight you moved, with how far you've come since the first time ("Up 20 lbs since Sep 1"). Every workout is written out under the graph, with your best marked.

## 0.4.105

- The sets on the watch's workout summary are written in larger text: as large as your watch's font allows while the longest set still fits on one line.
- New exercise: Chest supported machine row, under Back.

## 0.4.104

- The watch now shows the weight to lift before each set, so you can follow your plan. The rest screen's green button says "Next - 135 lb", its top line says which set is next ("Next Set: 3"), and the set screen says "Set 2 · 135 lb". The number is the weight your plan has for that set, or else the weight you used last.
- After you choose the first exercise of a workout, the rest screen opens first, with the weight to lift. Press Next when you're ready, just like every other set.

## 0.4.103

- Plank, side plank, wall sit, dead hang and glute bridge hold no longer ask for a weight. They only keep how long you held them.

## 0.4.102

- Editing a workout plan on the phone is quicker. Add exercise now stays at the top of the screen under Back, so you can add one without scrolling down.
- Tap an exercise's number to move it: type the place you want, such as 1 to put it first, instead of tapping ↑ many times.
- Tap a set's weight under Enter Set Weight to type the weight in, instead of stepping to it with − and +.

## 0.4.101

- Workout plans can now hold the weight for each set. On the phone, open a plan and tap Enter Set Weight under an exercise to set the weight of each of its sets; the first weight you enter fills in the rest. When you do that plan on the watch, the weight screen starts at the planned weight for that set.
- On the phone, the lists of workout plans and of past workouts are shorter: each plan's exercises and sets, and each workout's date and totals, fit on one line.
- When you edit a past workout on the phone, each set's weight, reps and time now sit between their − and + buttons, like the plan's set weights.

## 0.4.100

- Easier to see for everyone, including people with red-green colour blindness: the highlight that shows which button the select button will press is now yellow instead of red, and the green buttons are a darker green so their white text is easier to read.
- On the home screen, GO is wider and History, Units and Settings are bigger, with larger text and more space between them.
- On the Start workout screen, the lines under Quick start and Choose plan are larger and white, so they are easier to read.
- The first time you do an exercise, the weight and reps screen starts at 20 lbs (9 kg) and 10 reps instead of blank. Reps the watch counted still come first.
- The weight and reps wheel now works like the watch's own: larger numbers are below, so the down button goes up. The wheel has an outline so it stands apart from the − and + buttons, which are bigger, and the numbers either side and the unit are easier to read.

## 0.4.99

- Strava now shows a heart rate graph for your workout, through every set and rest, instead of nothing. The watch keeps a reading every 5 seconds. With "Heart rate with screen off" on in Settings the graph carries on while the screen is off; without it there are gaps while the screen sleeps.
- Buttons respond straight away when a screen opens. Before, the weight and reps boxes and the wheel's − and + buttons ignored taps for a moment after their screen came up.

## 0.4.98

- Strava no longer repeats your exercises and sets in the activity's description, since they already show in Strava's own exercise list. The description now just has your plan, anything you skipped, and the workout's totals. It now ends "Logged with the Zepp OS Weight Lifting app by Avour Labs LLC" and a link to avourlabs.com.

## 0.4.97

- You now get 7 free workouts (down from 14), then you can choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or pay once for lifetime use. Scan the code on the watch with your phone's camera, choose, and pay. There is no account to make and nothing to type: the watch unlocks by itself a few seconds later. Cancel a subscription any time from the link in your receipt.
- Got a new phone? The Unlock screen has a second code: scan it and enter the email you paid with, and what you bought moves to the new phone.
- Settings has a new "Heart rate with screen off" switch, so you can turn on background heart rate any time, even if you skipped it the first time.
- Larger, easier-to-read text on the Unlock, Train safely and Heart rate screens.

## 0.4.96

- The Test workouts buttons on the phone's Export and import page, which added made-up workouts, are gone.

## 0.4.95

- When setting the weight or reps, swiping left or right no longer leaves the screen, so a stray swipe while turning the wheel can't throw away your change. Use Done to save, or the back button to go back without saving.

## 0.4.94

- Workouts uploaded to Strava now show as real exercises in Strava's Exercises list, each with its sets, reps and weight, and Strava adds up the total volume. The written list of sets is still in the activity's description.

## 0.4.93

- Swiping in from the left edge of the watch no longer closes the app during a set, on the summary after a workout, or on the first safety screen. During a set the swipe does nothing; on the summary it goes back to Home. Swiping on Home still closes the app, and the back button works as before.

## 0.4.92

- Export and import on the phone (Zepp app, Weight Lifting, Export and import). Your workout history and workout plans are saved together in one backup file. Tap Show as text to copy it or email it to yourself; where your phone allows it, Share and Copy buttons appear too.
- To import, tap Import a backup, paste the backup into the large box, then tap Import. You can also give the web address of a backup instead. Workouts and plans you already have are kept as they are, so importing the same file twice adds nothing, and Undo the last import takes it all away again.

## 0.4.91

- Each workout plan on the phone's Workout Plans page now has Delete beside it, just like Workout History: tap Delete, then Sure? to remove the plan. You can still delete a plan from inside it too.
- The Workout Plans page on the phone has larger text. Making a new plan now starts with an Enter plan name button. Type the name, tap OK, and it shows with Next under it to go on to adding exercises. A plan's own page has Change plan name.
- The exercise list on the phone (when adding to a plan, or changing a set's exercise) has larger text and colour: exercises are green buttons, muscle groups pale green.
- Buttons on the phone that open something (Workout Plans, Workout History, each workout, each plan and each muscle group) have a large › on the right, and Workout Plans and Workout History stand out with larger names.
- A plan's page is tidier: each exercise has its name, then one row with its sets (− and +), ↑ and ↓ to move it, and ✕ to remove it.

## 0.4.90

- Home now shows how many free workouts you have left ("12 free workouts left"), so you always know before the app asks to be unlocked. Once it is unlocked, the line goes away.
- History shows 20 workouts at a time, on the watch and on the phone, with Next page and Previous page buttons to see the rest, so it opens quickly however many workouts you have.
- If you leave the app in the middle of a workout and do not come back to it for 30 minutes, the watch stops measuring your heart rate in the background, to save the battery. Opening the app again picks the workout up where you left it, and heart rate starts again.
- Fixed: tapping Disconnect on the phone's Strava box while a workout was uploading could leave Strava connected, or say it needed reconnecting.

## 0.4.89

- Workout plans. Make a plan on your phone (Zepp app, Weight Lifting, Workout Plans): tap New plan, give it a name, then choose the exercises you want to do and how many sets of each. On the watch, GO now asks whether to Quick start (as before) or Choose plan.
- With a plan, the exercise list starts with Remaining Exercises: each exercise with how many sets are left. Once all its sets are done it moves to the bottom of that list and is crossed out, and you can still pick it for another set.
- After the last planned set of an exercise, the rest screen's Start goes straight to the next exercise of the plan, in the order you set on the phone. You can change the order there with Move up and Move down.
- A tidier phone page: the disclaimer comes first and folds away once you accept it (tap it to read it again), Strava takes one line once connected, and your workout plans and workout history each have a page of their own.
- On the phone, the Back and Cancel buttons at the top of a screen now stay in place while you scroll, so they are always easy to find.
- Fixed: connecting Strava on a fresh install could show an error under "Connected to Strava" until the first workout uploaded.
- When you finish the last set of a plan, the watch says "Plan complete" with the plan's name. Choose Continue to keep lifting without the plan, or End to finish the workout.
- The workout summary (on the watch, the phone and Strava) names the plan, says where you did fewer sets than planned, and lists the exercises you skipped. You can finish early at any time, as before.

## 0.4.88

- You now get 14 free workouts before the app asks to be unlocked, up from 7. If you had already reached 7, you can start workouts again until you reach 14.

## 0.4.87

- The rest screen now shows how many sets you have done in the workout ("Sets 3"), and the green button says which set of that exercise is next, for example "Start 2nd Set of Dumbbell curl".
- The set number on the set screen and the Weight & reps screen now counts sets of that exercise, so your first squats after three sets of curls are Set 1, not Set 4.

## 0.4.86

- New Settings page, opened from Home, with the Isometric (Holds) Timer sound and a new Screen on setting. It opens once the first time you start the app after installing or updating, so you know they are there.
- The Isometric (Holds) Timer now vibrates by default. If you had picked Voice, Beeps or Silent, your choice is kept.
- Screen on: between sets the screen now stays on for as long as set on the watch (Display > Screen-on Duration > General), or you can choose 30 seconds to 3 minutes.
- During a set the screen always stays on, whatever this setting. The watch only sends motion readings while the screen is on, so reps would stop being counted, and it closes the app soon after the screen goes off, which would stop the 30-second cues during a hold.
- Fixed: on the workout summary, pressing the top right button once now presses Done whenever Done is on screen.

## 0.4.85

- New exercises: Machine shoulder press (Shoulders) and Machine seated row (Back).

## 0.4.84

- Heart rate now keeps tracking when the screen turns off, so it shows right away when you look again.

## 0.4.83

- Now works on square watches, such as the Amazfit Active 2 (Square).
